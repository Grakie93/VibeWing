# VibeWing Brand Guidelines

The source code is available under the MIT License. The VibeWing name, butterfly logo, product identity, and official distribution status are separate from the source-code license.

## You may

- Refer to VibeWing accurately in articles, reviews, integrations, and compatibility statements.
- State that a project is based on or compatible with VibeWing.
- Use screenshots of the official application for truthful documentation or commentary.

## You may not

- Present a modified build, fork, service, or installer as an official VibeWing release.
- Use the VibeWing name or butterfly logo in a way that suggests endorsement, partnership, or ownership by Grakie93 without permission.
- Use the official icon as the primary identity of an independently distributed fork.

## Forks

Forks may use the MIT-licensed code. Publicly distributed modified products should use a distinct product name and icon, preserve required copyright and license notices, and clearly state that they are unofficial forks.

## Official releases

Only artifacts published by Grakie93 through the `Grakie93/VibeWing` repository or another channel explicitly identified there are official VibeWing releases.

These guidelines describe the project's intended brand policy and are not a trademark-registration claim. For brand-permission requests, contact [grakie93@gmail.com](mailto:grakie93@gmail.com).
